The Fine Print

Terms & Conditions

Clear policies so we're on the same beat from day one.

Booking Policy

All bookings are confirmed only after receiving a signed agreement and the required deposit. Dates are reserved on a first-come, first-served basis and cannot be held without a deposit.

Cancellation Policy

Cancellations made 30+ days before the event are eligible for a partial refund of the deposit. Cancellations within 14 days of the event are non-refundable due to reserved scheduling.

Payment Terms

A 40% deposit secures your date, with the remaining balance due 7 days before the event. We accept bank transfer, card and UPI. Late payments may affect event scheduling.

Equipment Responsibility

All equipment remains the property of DJ BEATS. The client is responsible for any damage caused by guests. A safe, covered and powered performance area must be provided.

Privacy Statement

Any personal details shared with us are used solely for event coordination and are never sold or shared with third parties. Event media may be used for promotion only with your consent.
