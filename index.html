Who We Are

Fine Arts DJ Amplifier has been a landmark name for DJ and audio equipment in New Usmanpur, Delhi since 1994. What began as a specialist amplifier dealership has grown into a complete sound destination for DJs, event companies, banquet halls and studios.

From powerful amplifiers and thundering subwoofers to precision mixers and stage lighting, every product is hand-picked for reliability — and our in-house repair and setup expertise means you're never left stranded before a show.
